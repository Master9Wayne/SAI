Use the education-software corpus under `docs/ingested/education/` as comparative evidence. Separate source-observed facts from project-specific reasoning. Do not infer a named SDLC methodology unless a source explicitly names it. Treat public documentation as evidence of documented practice, not proof of internal execution. Flag missing jurisdiction, child-safety, privacy, accessibility, institutional-policy, and procurement details as `NEEDS-VALIDATION`. Final SDLC selection requires human approval by the product owner and relevant institutional privacy, safeguarding, security, accessibility, and release stakeholders.
